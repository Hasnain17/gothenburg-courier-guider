3 · Guidance

  • Run both apps at once. Accept one order, then decline the other app's offers until you drop off. Avoid holding two orders from the same hour unless the restaurants are next to each other.
  • Wait where restaurants cluster, not at home. Brunnsparken, Haga, Linnégatan and Avenyn give short pickups and short drops.
  • Judge an offer by total time, not distance. A short ride with a slow kitchen is worse than a slightly longer one with a quick pickup. Decline long rides across the river at dinner.
  • Bad weather = more orders. Rain, cold and snow push demand up. If you can ride safely, those are often your best hours.
  • Dead time (14:00–16:30): eat, charge your phone, check the bike, or reposition toward Linné/Chalmers before the dinner rush starts.
  • Use the apps' own demand hints. Both apps show busy areas or boosts. Trust them over this page when they disagree.
  • Safety: helmet, lights, and slow down on wet tram tracks. Keep insurance and your paperwork in order for self-employed courier work.

All ratings and times are general patterns for a city like Gothenburg, not live data. Test for a week, note your orders per hour per spot, and keep the winners.
